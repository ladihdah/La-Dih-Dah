Candle Care

Before you light

  • Trim the wood wick to about 1/8 in (3 mm) before each use.
  • On the first burn, allow the melt pool to reach toward the vessel edges.

While it burns

  • Burn no longer than 4 hours at a time.
  • Keep away from children, pets, drafts and anything flammable.
  • Never leave a burning candle unattended.
  • Stop use when about 1/2 in (13 mm) of wax remains.

380 g candle: approx. 60–70 hours. Burn time varies with use and conditions.
