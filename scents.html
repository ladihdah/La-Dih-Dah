Two Moods, One Ritual

FRESH · WOODSY · COMFORTING

Winterscape

The world goes quiet.

Crisp winter air, snow-covered woods and the comforting warmth of home. Fresh and atmospheric without feeling overly sweet — the hush that arrives after the first snowfall.

Crisp bergamot · iced rhubarb · winter jasmine · white lavender · benzoin resin · sandalwood

For cold nights, warm rooms and nowhere else you need to be.

WARM · RICH · INTIMATE

Sensual Amber

Stay awhile.

Warm, enveloping and quietly seductive. A soft, sophisticated warmth fills the room — rich and intimate rather than overpowering. Made for low lights, slow evenings and moments you do not want to rush.

Forest fruits · plum · bergamot · orange blossom · white rose · lotus · amber · vanilla · musk

Dim the lights. Light the candle. Stay awhile.
